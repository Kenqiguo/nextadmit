## 30-second introduction

NextAdmit is an AI-assisted web prototype I developed for students exploring college transfers, especially international students. It presents applicant backgrounds alongside admission results and includes a searchable directory of 207 universities. I defined the product requirements and used AI to help implement it in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I also tested submission persistence. The demo is on GitHub Pages. Its built-in profiles are fictional, and submissions stay in the visitor's browser.
